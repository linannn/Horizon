### [AI 代理在 OpenRouter 上超越人类，Token 用量激增 14 倍](https://the-decoder.com/ai-is-becoming-ais-biggest-customer-as-agentic-token-usage-jumps-14x-on-openrouter/) ⭐️ 6.0/10
